# Code of conduct

Everyone working on CWR, in the repository, in reviews and in any channel about it, is expected to:

- be respectful and professional, and assume good intent;
- critique work, not people; review comments name the clause, the line and the fix;
- report results honestly, including negative and inconclusive ones, and never overstate what
  evidence shows;
- keep other people's credentials, data and private communication private;
- welcome questions and make room for people new to the project.

Unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing someone's
private information, and deliberately misrepresenting results or evidence.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Sidiora Labs Inc. through your project contact. Reports are
handled confidentially by Sidiora Labs, who decide on the response, from a private warning to
removal of access. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
