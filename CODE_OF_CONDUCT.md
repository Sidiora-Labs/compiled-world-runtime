# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in CWR: in the repository, in issues and reviews, and
in any channel about the project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional, and assume good intent.
- Critique the work, not the person. A review comment names the clause, the line and the fix.
- Report results honestly, including negative and inconclusive ones. Never overstate what the
  evidence shows.
- Keep other people's credentials, data and private communication private.
- Welcome questions, and make room for people who are new to the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting results or evidence.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to Sidiora Labs Inc. through your project contact. Sidiora Labs
handles each report confidentially and decides the response, which ranges from a private warning
to removal of access.

A suspected vulnerability is not a conduct matter. Report it as described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
